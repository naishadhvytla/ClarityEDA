FAQ

Questions, answered

Is my data private?

Yes. Your file is analysed inside your browser — nothing is sent anywhere while you explore. Only when you press Save is the cleaned dataset stored, in a private folder that only your account can read.

Which files can I upload?

CSV, TSV, Excel (.xlsx, .xls) and JSON, up to 50 MB. The first row should contain column names; Clarity skips title rows in Excel sheets automatically.

How does Clarity “understand” my columns?

It reads both the column names and the values. revenue_inr becomes a money amount in ₹, city a location, churned (Yes/No) an outcome, order_id an identifier that shouldn't be added up. If it gets one wrong, change it on the Columns page and the report updates instantly.

Is this AI? Can I trust the numbers?

Every sentence is generated from a statistic computed on your data — totals, shares, averages, growth, effect sizes. Nothing is invented. Where a difference could be chance, the report says so.

How many analyses can I save?

Each free account keeps 2 saved analyses. Delete one to free a slot — you can always export the report and cleaned data first.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Every page is designed for small screens, and exported reports print cleanly to PDF.
